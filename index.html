What I do

Three things, done properly.

Most local businesses don't need a big agency. They need a site that loads fast, says the right thing, and doesn't quietly break six months later.

Design

Make it look like you

A layout built around what your customers came to find, not a stock template with your logo dropped in.

  • Colors and type that match your sign
  • Photos of your actual shop
  • Readable on a phone in the parking lot
Build

Put it online

I build the pages, test them on real phones, and handle the domain and hosting setup. You never have to log into a control panel.

  • Images compressed so it isn't sluggish on cell data
  • Click-to-call and click-for-directions
  • Google Business Profile set up and connected
Maintain

Keep it current

New hours, new menu, new prices. Text me the change and it's live the same week, usually the same day.

  • Content updates without a ticket system
  • Backups and security patches
  • A real person who answers

How it works

From first call to live site.

Four steps. Two to three weeks for most projects.

01  /  Week 1

We talk

Twenty minutes, free, at your shop or over the phone. What you sell, who buys it, what you want the site to do.

02  /  Week 1

You see a draft

A real page you can click through, not a slideshow. You tell me what's wrong with it and I fix it.

03  /  Week 2–3

It goes live

I connect the domain, set up hosting, and get you listed so you turn up in local search. You own everything.

04  /  Ongoing

I keep it running

Updates, fixes, and seasonal changes. Optional, month to month, cancel whenever.
